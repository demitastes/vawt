Summary

Nestled in the heart of historical Halifax County, Virginia, Springfield Distillery stands as a testament to craftsmanship and tradition. Our distillery is located at Springfield, a house dating back to 1842, surrounded by scenic porch sipping country views, offering the perfect setting for creating our exceptional spirits.

At Springfield Distillery, we're proud to be recognized for our commitment to quality and craftsmanship. Our handcrafted spirits have received numerous awards, showcasing our dedication to creating exceptional whiskeys, bourbons, and specialty spirits. Explore our accolades and see why Springfield Distillery is celebrated by industry experts and enthusiasts alike.

Springfield Distillery is proudly women-owned, led by Kelly Gordon. Since 2016, Kelly's passion and dedication have driven the creation of our exceptional handcrafted spirits. Her vision and leadership embody the spirit of innovation and quality, making Springfield Distillery a standout in Virginia's distilling industry. Join us in celebrating the achievements of women in craft distilling.
