Summary

We are architects and craftsmen who started a whiskey company. We like things old and natural: wood, steel, people, buildings, landscapes, souls, or hell...any good story. We prefer analog over the digital, the simple over the complex, the slow over the frantic. We don't think we're alone - yearning for more long days in the saddle, on the porch, fishing a remote river, early morning adventures and long dinners with loved ones. We think whiskey and architecture are key and compatible ingredients to life. Our straight bourbon is single barrel with a unique story number leather band. Celebrate that experience, save the leather, remember that day, that moment, that smile, that conversation. Because for us there is nothing more valuable than your STORY.

To make a good bourbon you have to start with good chemistry, equipment, and raw distillate. We proudly source from some of the finest distillers in America - a proven secret for some of the finest whiskies in the world. From here we add value in the only way we know how: through our Architecture, with our Love of nature, with the hour glass of time, and by ONLY offering single barrel products. Each barrel has it's unique story to tell - we never blend. We don’t add any frivolous flavors to our whiskey. We keep our recipe simple. We want our whiskey consumed over a couple of ice cubes and the lost art of conversation. Our bottles are purposefully smaller - to be enjoyed in a single sitting or adventure. Each bottle is handcrafted by the owners - wrapped in a uniquely branded leather strap. We want you to enjoy the time, remember that day, that person, that story - forever. To us the value of whiskey is one part chemistry and art the other part story and kinship - to us the most valuable element of any life well lived.
