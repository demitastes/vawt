Battle Standard 142 Gin
Navy Strength
Battle Standard 142 Gin Navy Strength derives its name, “Navy Strength”, from the British Navy’s practice of proofing gin rations to 114 proof. This was done so if spilled on the ship’s gunpowder, the gunpowder could still be fired in battle.

ABV: 57% (114 PROOF)

Tasting Notes

Don’t let its higher proof fool you, Battle Standard 142 Gin Navy Strength is surprisingly smooth with big, bold, and vibrant notes of juniper, citrus, and cinnamon.

How to Drink

Battle Standard 142 Gin Navy Strength works wonderfully in a Classic Gin and Tonic or Gin Martini. We also recommend the Gin Lime Rickey, Gin Kosmo, Cranberry Gin Fizz or any cocktail that benefits from a bold, delicious gin.

High Rye Bourbon

Bare Knuckle American Whiskey
The Blue Ridge is home to some of the oldest distilling traditions in America. Recognizing the spirit of this Virginian tradition we make this double-distilled whiskey, aged for one day, we call Bare Knuckle American Whiskey.

ABV: 45% (90 PROOF)

Tasting Notes

Unlike “moonshine” or corn whiskeys, Bare Knuckle American Whiskey is made with 60% wheat, 30% rye, and 10% malted barley. This creates a refreshing taste with sweet upfront flavor from the wheat and a peppery finish from the rye.

How to Drink

Bare Knuckle American Whiskey is outstanding in a variety of craft cocktails. It can be used in both traditional and non-traditional whiskey drinks alike, such as a Manassas Mule, Moonshine Margarita, Whiskey Sour, or Whiskey Ginger.
